How to Play Event Horizon

🎯 The Objective

Event Horizon is a cosmic tactical numbers game played on a 36-space pyramid. Outscore Orbit (the Computer) by strategically positioning your high numbers so they are drawn into the Black Hole when the final space collapses!

🚀 How Turns Work

  • 1. Select an Empty Space: Click any available circle on the pyramid board.
  • 2. Pick Your Number: Choose a number (1 to 9) from your tray at the bottom. You each start with a quota of 2 tokens per number.
  • 3. Place the Number: Click "Place Number" to lock in your move.
  • 4. Orbit's Move: Orbit will analyze the board and place its number.

🌌 The Black Hole & Scoring

  • The Collapse: When only 1 last empty space remains on the board, that space becomes the Black Hole (Event Horizon)!
  • Gravitational Pull: The Black Hole consumes all adjacent neighboring spaces (same row, row above, and row below — up to 6 surrounding tokens).
  • Scoring: Only tokens consumed by the Black Hole count toward scoring! Your final score is the sum of all your consumed token values.
  • Winning: The player with the highest total score from consumed tokens wins the game!

💡 Tactical Pro Tip

Pay close attention to where open spaces are grouping together. Cluster your highest-value numbers (7, 8, 9) around predicted collapse zones, and force Orbit to waste its numbers on outer fringes!
